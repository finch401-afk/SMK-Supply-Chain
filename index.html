Why work with SMK

What we do differently on each project.

Four practices that reduce surprises when you buy from China for the first time, or the fiftieth.

01You deal with one contact

One person at SMK follows your project across suppliers, inspection, freight, warehousing and delivery. You do not have to relay information between five companies.

02Specifications are settled before production

Dimensions, materials, samples, packaging and shipping method are confirmed early, so the factory builds to what you approved, not to a guess.

03Goods are checked before they leave China

Pre-shipment inspection with photo and video reports, and loading supervision, so problems surface while they can still be fixed at the factory.

04Canadian delivery is planned from the start

From Calgary, we line up warehousing and delivery around when you need the goods, and compare suppliers, consolidate orders and plan transport to manage landed cost.

Questions

Common questions before a first project.

Something else? Email info@smksupplychain.com or call 825-488-5555.

I already have a supplier in China. Can you work with them?

Yes. You can choose only the services you need. For example, SMK can follow up on production, arrange an inspection before shipment, and coordinate freight, warehousing and delivery for goods from a supplier you already use.

What do you need from me to quote a custom product?

Whatever you have: a drawing, a sample, photos or a written description. The most useful details are dimensions, materials, finish, any load or performance requirements, branding and packaging needs, quantity, target price and when you need the goods.

Can I use SMK only for freight, warehousing or delivery?

Yes. Each service can be used on its own. Tell us where the goods are, what they are (cartons, pallets or oversized cargo) and where they need to go.

Do you handle customs clearance?

SMK coordinates the customs process as part of your shipment: the commercial documents, the information needed for clearance and the communication between the parties involved. SMK is not a licensed customs broker.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimums are set by each factory and depend on the product. We review MOQ and lead time with suppliers during sourcing so you can compare options before committing.

How is your work priced?

Pricing depends on the product, specifications, quantity, route and market conditions, so each project is quoted after we understand what you need. Contact us with your requirements and we will explain the costs for your project.

How quickly will I hear back?

We reply to new enquiries within 24 hours. If something is urgent, call us at 825-488-5555.
