Power cable · Government partnership project

Power cable sourced in China for an Edmonton project

Client need

A client needed a batch of power cable from China for a government partnership project in Edmonton.

SMK’s work

  • Sourced the power cable in China
  • Ocean freight in two 20-ft containers: the cable is heavy, so 20-ft containers were used to stay within weight limits
  • Unloading the cable reels at SMK’s warehouse in Calgary
  • Staging the reels at the warehouse
  • Delivery to the project in Edmonton

Result

2 × 20-ftcontainers of power cable

Delivered from China to the Edmonton project, through one contact at SMK.

Heavy equipment · Oversized cargo

Tracked excavator: flat-rack export preparation

Client need

A full-size Sumitomo SH330LC tracked excavator had to travel as oversized cargo on an open platform rather than inside a standard container.

SMK’s work

  • Positioned the excavator on an open flat-rack platform
  • Timber blocking under the undercarriage
  • Multi-point steel-wire lashing across the undercarriage and boom, to limit movement during handling and ocean transit
  • Condition and securing photo record before dispatch

Result

The excavator was loaded, secured and documented before dispatch. Routing details are withheld for confidentiality.
