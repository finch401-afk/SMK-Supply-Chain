About SMK Supply Chain

S.M.K Supply Chain is a China sourcing and supply chain partner based in Calgary, Alberta, working with Canadian businesses that buy products made in China.

What we do

We connect the China side of a purchase with the Canadian side.

Buying from China usually means dealing with a supplier, an inspector, a freight forwarder, a customs process, a warehouse and a trucking company, each with its own contact and its own part of the information.

SMK coordinates those steps as one project. On the China side, that means sourcing, production follow-up and inspection. On the Canadian side, it means freight and customs coordination, warehousing at SMK’s own warehouse in Calgary, and delivery. Clients can use the full route or only the services they need.

Who we work with

Canadian businesses without a sourcing team in China.

  • Importers and wholesalers buying stock or supplies
  • Contractors sourcing materials, equipment or fixtures for a project
  • Manufacturers that need parts or custom components
  • Commercial project buyers, such as interiors and fit-outs

How we work

Practical habits, not slogans.

01One accountable contact

Your project has one SMK contact from the first request to final delivery.

02Confirm early

Specifications, samples, quality, packaging and shipping are confirmed before production, when changes are cheapest.

03Show the evidence

Inspection photo and video reports, and photo records of loading, so you can see the goods before they ship.

04Be clear about prices

Past project prices are shown as what they were: results from specific orders, not promises for the next one.
