01

China Sourcing

Find manufacturers for the product you want to buy, compare real quotations, and approve samples before you place an order.

Request a quote for this service

Who it is for

  • Companies buying a product from China for the first time
  • Buyers paying distributor prices in Canada who want to compare a factory-direct option
  • Teams without sourcing staff or contacts in China

What SMK coordinates

  • Identifying candidate manufacturers for your product
  • Supplier verification before you commit
  • Collecting and comparing quotations
  • MOQ and lead-time review
  • Samples for your approval
  • Price negotiation and order coordination

Send us for a quote

  • Product description, photos or a link to a similar item
  • Key specifications: size, material, grade, finish
  • Order quantity, or expected annual volume
  • Target price, or what you pay today (optional)
  • Delivery city in Canada and the date you need the goods

Missing some of this? Send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

02

Product Development & Custom Manufacturing

Turn a drawing, a sample or a concept into an OEM or ODM product made to your dimensions, materials and branding.

Request a quote for this service

Who it is for

  • Businesses with their own design, drawing or sample
  • Private-label and logo-printed products
  • Equipment, fixtures or furniture that must fit a specific site or load

What SMK coordinates

  • Matching your product to factories with the right process
  • Drawing, dimension and load review with the factory
  • Samples and prototypes
  • Material, finish and packaging choices
  • Logo and print approval
  • Production follow-up until the order is complete

Send us for a quote

  • Drawings or sketches with dimensions, or a physical sample
  • Materials, finish and colour
  • Load, performance or compliance requirements, if any
  • Branding and packaging requirements
  • Quantity, target price and required delivery date

Missing some of this? Send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

03

Quality Control

Find problems while the goods are still at the factory, not after they arrive in Canada.

Request a quote for this service

Who it is for

  • Buyers who have approved a sample and need the production order to match it
  • Importers working with an existing supplier who want an independent check
  • Orders where packaging and loading matter: fragile, finished or heavy goods

What SMK coordinates

  • Pre-shipment inspection against your approved specifications or sample
  • Photo and video reports you can review before shipment
  • Packaging and labelling checks
  • Loading supervision

Send us for a quote

  • Approved specifications, drawings or sample photos
  • Purchase order quantity and packing details
  • Factory location and expected ready date
  • The checks that matter most to you

Missing some of this? Send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

04

Freight & Customs Coordination

A coordinated route from the supplier in China to Canada, including consolidation and customs coordination.

Request a quote for this service

Who it is for

  • Importers whose goods are ready, or nearly ready, at a supplier in China
  • Buyers with several suppliers who want one consolidated shipment
  • Oversized or heavy cargo that does not fit a standard container

What SMK coordinates

  • FCL (full container) and LCL (shared container) ocean freight
  • Air freight for urgent shipments
  • Consolidating goods from several suppliers
  • Flat-rack shipments for oversized equipment
  • Break-bulk shipping on heavy-lift vessels for equipment too large for any container
  • Coordinating the commercial documents and information needed for customs clearance

Send us for a quote

  • Commercial invoice and packing list, or a product list
  • Number of cartons or pallets, with dimensions and weights
  • Pickup location in China and the ready date
  • Destination in Canada
  • Incoterms and HS codes, if you know them

Missing some of this? Send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

SMK coordinates customs clearance with the parties involved in your shipment. SMK is not a licensed customs broker.

05

Warehousing

Prepare imported goods for storage, sale and distribution in Canada.

Request a quote for this service

Who it is for

  • Importers who need a container unloaded and goods held before distribution
  • Businesses that want deliveries released on their own schedule
  • Orders that need labelling or palletizing after arrival

What SMK coordinates

  • Container de-stuffing (unloading)
  • Indoor storage in SMK’s 20,000 sq ft Calgary warehouse
  • Labelling and palletizing
  • Inventory support
  • Releasing goods for final delivery

Send us for a quote

  • Container size or number of pallets
  • Product type and any handling requirements
  • Labelling or palletizing needed
  • Expected storage period
  • How and when goods will go out

Missing some of this? Send what you have. We will ask for the rest.

SMK operates its own 20,000 sq ft warehouse in Calgary for indoor storage. Outdoor yard storage is not available. For storage rates, contact us with your volume and storage period.
